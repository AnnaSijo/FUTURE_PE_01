# Business Profile

## Business Name

Koda Café Restaurant

## Location

Marathakkara, Thrissur, Kerala

## Industry

Café & Multi-Cuisine Restaurant

## Business Overview

Koda Café Restaurant is a modern rooftop café and restaurant offering Arabian, Chinese, Continental, and Indian cuisine. The restaurant provides a welcoming atmosphere for students, families, couples, professionals, and tourists.

## Target Audience

* Students
* Families
* Couples
* Professionals
* Tourists

## Key Services

* Dine-in
* Takeaway
* Delivery
* Rooftop Dining
* Table Reservations

## Unique Selling Points

* Rooftop Dining
* Multi-Cuisine Menu
* Live Music
* Family-Friendly Atmosphere
* Ample Parking

## Website Goals

* Improve online presence
* Increase reservations
* Increase customer engagement
* Showcase menu and ambience
* Build brand awareness
